## 1. In one minute
After this plan `GET /api/v1/receipts/{id}/pdf` answers an A5 PDF receipt with the company logo, the modes, the total in words and the invoices it paid.

## 2. Touches / leaves out
Touches: the `payment` module (a PDF model builder and an embedded A5 template, one endpoint), reusing the template renderer, the HTML-to-PDF renderer and the company identity capability (no new package). No schema change.
Leaves out: the screen button (payment#08).

## 3. Approach
Same shape as the invoice PDF: a model of pre-formatted, HTML-encoded values filled into a Scriban template and rendered by the existing browser renderer; the amount in words reuses the sales helper; a cancelled receipt gets a CANCELLED mark.

## 4. Tasks (at most 8)

- [ ] 2. The A5 template (header, modes, allocations, words, bank, signature).
- [ ] 3. The endpoint and its roles; build at 0 warnings.
