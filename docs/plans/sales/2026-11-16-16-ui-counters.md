## 1. In one minute
After this plan the Owner and Staff have a **Counters** menu: the list of counters with search and filters, the Owner adds, edits, closes and opens a counter in the side panel, and a counter row has "Open billing" (the billing screen arrives with sales#18). Open `/<company>/counters`.

## 2. Touches / leaves out
Touches: `libs/shared-models` (counter model), `libs/api-services` (`CounterService`), `apps/member` (`pages/counters`: list, detail panel, form; routes `counters`, `counters/new`, `counters/:id`, `counters/:id/edit`; the menu entry for Owner and Staff), `assets/i18n/en.json`.
Leaves out: the billing screen, the bills list and the summary (their own UI plans).

## 3. Approach
It follows the vendor and school screens (platform U10: list beside a panel, X to close, the id in the address). The form has the school (only schools that sell at the counter), the session choice from the school's session choice, the dates and the name (with a suggested `<school> Counter <session>`); Staff see the list and the panel but no Add, Edit, Close or Open. Each state shows loading, empty, error with retry and success; all texts from the catalogue.
