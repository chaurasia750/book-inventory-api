## 1. In one minute
After this plan the Owner can pay a school's credit back: `POST /api/v1/refunds` with the school, the amount and the mode (Cash, UPI or Bank). The ledger records the money going out, the school's balance rises by the amount, and the refund gets a number `RF/2026-27/0001` and an A5 **refund slip** PDF to hand to the school: `GET /api/v1/refunds/{id}/pdf`. A refund is never more than what the company owes the school. The Owner can cancel a refund.

## 2. Touches / leaves out
Touches: `Domain/Sales` (`SchoolRefund` root, mode and status enums, events), `Application/Sales` (a record service, a cancel service, a query service, a slip PDF service with its model builder and one embedded A5 template like the payment receipt), the finance capability call for the school's balance and the posting, `Infrastructure` (configuration, one migration `Sales_SchoolRefund`), `Api/Controllers/Sales`, the message catalogue, `docs/Code_Map.md`.
Leaves out: the screens (sales#26), a refund to a customer or a vendor, the credit note PDF, proof attachments, cheque refunds.

## 3. Approach
The refund is its own aggregate: the amount, the mode rules and the status live in the domain. The record service reads the school's balance through the finance capability, refuses an amount above the credit, takes the next number from the code series and posts one ledger transaction (debit `SCHOOL_RECEIVABLE`, credit `CASH` or `BANK`) in the request transaction; the cancel posts the exact opposite. The slip is rendered on demand through the platform template port and PDF renderer, like the counter receipt.
