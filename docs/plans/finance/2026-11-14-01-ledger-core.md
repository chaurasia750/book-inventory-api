## 1. In one minute
After this plan the company has a real double-entry ledger: later modules (Invoice, Payment, Return, Purchase) post a document's balanced lines through one capability, a document can never be posted twice, and a cancel posts the exact opposite. A party's balance is the sum of its lines. Nothing visible yet; it is seen working through a throw-away scratch caller that posts an invoice-like document for a school and reverses it.

## 2. Touches / leaves out

## 3. Approach
`LedgerTransaction` derives from `CompanyOwnedRoot` and is created complete by one factory that checks every rule of R3 and R8 (two lines or more, one side per line, positive amounts with at most two decimals, a party exactly on a party account of the same type, debits equal credits) and derives the financial year (R4); a reversal is built by the root from the original (`Reverse()`), so the opposite lines are computed in one place. Lines are `TransactionDomainBase`-style insert-only children, company-owned, and copy the date, year and session for the statement's filters. `ILedgerPosting` (Application) refuses a document already posted (store check plus the unique index), and `ReverseAsync` refuses an unknown or already reversed document; both run in the caller's transaction. Balances are summed from the lines (R7), never stored. Accounts and party types are enums stored as text with check constraints. The ledger rows are `[AuditIgnore]` (R14).
