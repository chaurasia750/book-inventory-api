## 1. Purpose and scope

`school` holds the schools a company sells to: who they are, how their year runs, how they buy, and the starting balance they owe. Orders, invoices, counters, ledgers and planning of later modules point at a school by id and never copy these values.

Feature **School master** (R1-R15): an Owner creates, edits, activates and deactivates the schools of their own company and keeps each school's class list; Staff can look the schools up; a Super Admin works with a company's schools only after entering that company ([specs/company.md](company.md) R13). A school belongs to exactly one company and no other company ever sees it ([specs/platform.md](platform.md) P18-P21).

Out of scope for this feature: Excel import of schools (FR-SCH-07, NFR-12: its own later feature, it needs an Excel package approved, Q9); deriving a school's session from a date and the changeable cut-off dates (BR-SES-04 to BR-SES-06, Q-05: they come with the first order feature, Q1); posting the opening balance into the school ledger (the Ledger module); class-wise book lists and planning; the screens of the Super Admin in the `admin` app (Q11).

## 2. Concepts and glossary

- **School**: one customer school of one company (R1).
- **Session type**: how the school's year runs: `APR_MAR` or `JAN_DEC` (R3). It is the school's own; the company's financial year is always April-March (BR-SES-01).
- **Sale type**: what the company sells to this school: `SCHOOL_ORDER`, `COUNTER_SALE` or `BOTH` (R4).
- **GST default**: whether a new order of this school starts with GST on (R5).
- **Class list**: the school's classes from Nursery to 12th, in order, editable per school (R6).
- **Opening balance**: what the school owed when the system was started (R7).

## 3. Business rules

**School master**
- **R1** A school belongs to exactly one company, the company of the session that creates it. Another company's schools are never read or changed (platform P18-P21); asking for one answers `404`, identical to an unknown id.
- **R2** A school has: **code** (generated by the system, never typed or changed, R15), **name** (required, 2-150 characters), address, contact person, mobile, email, GSTIN (optional) and state (FR-SCH-01). The state is one of a fixed list that holds only Bihar for now; a new school starts as Bihar, and adding another state later is a small change of the list (D6). The optional values are trimmed and empty becomes none.
- **R3** The **session type** is required, `APR_MAR` or `JAN_DEC` (FR-SCH-02). It can be changed while the school has no documents; once documents exist the later modules refuse the change (BR-SES-03), so this module only stores it.
- **R4** The **sale type** is required: `SCHOOL_ORDER`, `COUNTER_SALE` or `BOTH` (FR-SCH-03). A new school starts as `BOTH` (Q6).
- **R5** The **GST default** is a yes or no and starts as no (FR-SCH-04, BR-GST-02: GST only on the school's demand). It only sets the starting value of a later order; the order can still toggle it (BR-GST-03).
- **R6** A school has an ordered **class list**. A new school starts with Nursery, LKG, UKG and 1st to 12th (Q2); the Owner can add, rename, reorder and remove classes (FR-SCH-05). Class names are unique inside the school ignoring case; each class has an order number. A class that keeps its name keeps its row and id when the list is saved again, and a removed name can be added again later. Later modules that use a class guard its removal.
- **R7** The **opening balance** is an amount of at least zero with two decimals: what the school owed when the system was started (FR-SCH-06). Positive means the school owes the company (Q5). It is stored here; posting it into the school ledger belongs to the Ledger module.
- **R8** A school is `ACTIVE` or `INACTIVE` (FR-SCH-08). A new school is active. An inactive school keeps all its data and history but cannot be chosen for a new document (later modules); it can be activated again. A school is never deleted (Q7).
- **R9** The Owner creates, edits, activates, deactivates a school and edits its class list. Staff may only read the list and a school (to pick one when selling, [specs/company.md](company.md) D5, Q8) and see active schools only. Anyone else is refused (`403`).
- **R10** A Super Admin reaches a company's schools only while they have entered that company ([specs/company.md](company.md) R13); with no company entered there is nothing to read and a write is refused (platform P19, P20).
- **R11** The list is paged (page size capped at 200), searchable by name, code, contact person and mobile, filterable by status, sale type, session type and state, and sortable by name, code and created date (BRD §7.1: every list has search and filter).
- **R12** Every create and change is recorded who and when through the standard audit columns and the audit trail of `core` ([specs/core.md](core.md) A1-A9); this module adds no audit mechanism of its own.
- **R13** The mobile number is stored as digits only (10-15 digits), an Indian number with a leading `91` on 12 digits or `0` on 11 digits is stored without it, the same way `iam` stores mobile numbers (Q10). The email is checked for the usual shape and length.
- **R15** The **code** is generated when the school is created: `SCH-` and a number of at least four digits from a counter that belongs to the company (`SCH-0001`, `SCH-0002`, ...). A number is never given twice, also not after the school is deactivated, and two schools created at the same moment get different numbers. The code is unique inside the company and cannot be changed (D5). A `code` sent in a request is ignored.
- **R14** Changing a school's name, state or GSTIN does not touch documents already issued: later modules copy what they must keep (BRD §8 design decision 3, price and party snapshots).

## 4. Data

Schema `school`:
- `school` (aggregate root, company-owned, platform P18): `company_id` (not null), `code` (generated, R15), `name`, `address`, `contact_person`, `mobile`, `email`, `gstin`, `state`, `session_type`, `sale_type`, `is_gst_default`, `opening_balance_amount` (`numeric(18,2)`, not negative), `status`, plus the standard audit and concurrency columns. Unique index on `(company_id, code)`.
- The code counter is the platform's company code series (`core.company_code_series`, prefix `SCH`, [specs/platform.md](platform.md) P23), read and increased under a lock inside the creating transaction (R15); the school has no counter table of its own since platform#07.
- `school_class` (company-owned child of `school`, platform P18): `company_id`, `school_id`, `name`, `name_normalized`, `sort_order`. Unique on `(school_id, name_normalized)` among live rows; a removed class is soft-deleted.

`company_id` is a plain id with no foreign key across schemas (the `company` module guarantees it exists).

## 5. API contract (`/api/v1`)

All routes are company-scoped by the session (platform P19, P20); the company is never a request field.

| Method + route | Who | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `POST schools` | `OWNER`, `SUPER_ADMIN` (entered) | `{name, address?, contactPerson?, mobile?, email?, gstin?, state?, sessionType, saleType?, isGstDefault?, openingBalanceAmount?, classes?}` -> `201` with the generated `code`; `400` for invalid values (a `code` in the body is ignored); `403 core.company_required` with no company |
| `GET schools` | `OWNER`, `STAFF`, `SUPER_ADMIN` (entered) | paged list (R11); Staff sees active schools only |
| `GET schools/{id}` | `OWNER`, `STAFF`, `SUPER_ADMIN` (entered) | the school with its class list; Staff only an active school; `404` otherwise |
| `PUT schools/{id}` | `OWNER`, `SUPER_ADMIN` (entered) | edits R2-R5, R7 (not the code, not the status, not the classes) |
| `PUT schools/{id}/classes` | `OWNER`, `SUPER_ADMIN` (entered) | `{classes: [names in order]}` replaces the class list (R6) |
| `POST schools/{id}/deactivate`, `/activate` | `OWNER`, `SUPER_ADMIN` (entered) | R8; `409` when already in that status |

Errors are `ProblemDetails` with a message code from the module's catalogue. Validation failures are `400`.

## 6. User interface

Written in the UI plan; summary: in the company workspace (`member` app) the Owner gets a "Schools" menu: a list with search and filters, an add and edit form with the class list editor, and activate and deactivate; Staff get the same list read-only. Each state shows loading, empty, error with retry and success.

## 7. Events

Raised by the `School` aggregate (past tense, ids and values only): `SchoolCreated`, `SchoolDeactivated`, `SchoolReactivated`. Reactions: none in this feature.

## 8. Dependencies

`school` depends on `iam` for the current company (`ICurrentCompany`) and on `core` for the audit trail; it needs the company-owned data mechanism of the platform ([specs/platform.md](platform.md) P18-P22). Nothing depends on a school's code, only on its id.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

- **AC1** The Owner of company A creates a school: `201`, code `SCH-0001`, `ACTIVE`, state Bihar, the class list holds the 15 default classes, `opening_balance_amount` 0, `is_gst_default` false, sale type `BOTH`; the row's `company_id` is A (R1, R2, R4-R8, R15).
- **AC2** A second school in A gets `SCH-0002` and the first school in company B gets `SCH-0001`; a `code` sent in the body is ignored; deactivating `SCH-0002` and creating another gives `SCH-0003`; ten schools created in parallel get ten different codes (R15).
- **AC3** The Owner of B listing or opening A's school gets nothing or `404`, identical to an unknown id; a `companyId` in the query or body changes nothing (R1).
- **AC4** Editing a school changes only the sent values; a negative opening balance, a state outside the list (for example Delhi), a 1-character name and a wrong session type are `400`, and the code cannot be changed (R2, R3, R7, R15).
- **AC5** Replacing the class list with `["Nursery", "1st", "1st"]` is `400` (duplicate, R6); with `["Nursery", "1st", "2nd"]` the list is those three in that order.
- **AC6** Deactivating a school keeps it in the list under the inactive filter and hides it from Staff; activating restores it; deactivating twice is `409` (R8, R9).
- **AC7** Staff can list and open active schools but `POST`, `PUT`, `PUT classes`, deactivate and activate answer `403`; an anonymous caller `401` (R9).
- **AC8** A Super Admin with no company entered lists no schools and `POST schools` answers `403 core.company_required`; after entering A they list A's schools and can create one (R10).
- **AC9** The list is paged and capped at 200, and the search, status, sale type, session type and state filters and the sorts work; an unknown sort column is ignored (R11).
- **AC10** Every create and change of a school appears in the audit trail with the actor and company A (R12).
- **AC11** Mobile `+91 98765 00001`, `0 98765 00001` and `9876500001` are stored as `9876500001`; an invalid email is `400` (R13).

## 10. Decisions

Proposed by the assistant on the owner's request of 2026-11-14 and approved with the spec on 2026-11-14 (D5 and D6 are the owner's own answers).

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| D1 | New module `school` with its own schema. A school is a company-owned aggregate root; its classes are children of it. (proposed by the assistant) |
| D2 | The mechanism that scopes company-owned data (a `company_id` column, one global query filter, the company stamped from the session) is written as platform rules P18-P22 and built by plan platform#01, as decided in [specs/company.md](company.md) D8. (proposed by the assistant) |
| D3 | The school stores only its session type; the session of a date and the changeable cut-offs arrive with the first order feature (Q1). (proposed by the assistant) |
| D4 | Schools are deactivated, never deleted (R8), the same way as accounts and companies. (proposed by the assistant) |
| D5 | The school code is generated by the system as `SCH-0001`, `SCH-0002`, ... from a counter of the company, never typed or changed (Q3, owner; the prefix is fixed for now). |
| D6 | The business runs only in Bihar for now, so the state list holds only Bihar and a new school starts as Bihar; the CGST/SGST against IGST logic (BR-GST-05) and further states come when a second state is needed (Q4, owner). |
| D7 | Answered "recommended" by the owner on 2026-11-14: the session derivation and the cut-offs come with the first order feature (Q1); the default class list is Nursery, LKG, UKG, 1st to 12th (Q2); the opening balance is zero or more, positive means the school owes the company (Q5); the sale type of a new school is `BOTH` (Q6); a school is never deleted, only deactivated (Q7); Staff only read active schools (Q8); Excel import is a later feature that needs an Excel library approved (Q9); mobile and email are checked like in `iam` (Q10); the Super Admin's school screens in `admin` come later (Q11). |
