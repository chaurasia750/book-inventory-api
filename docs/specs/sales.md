## 1. Purpose and scope

`sales` holds what the company sells to schools: the **school order** (a draft that becomes a confirmed order) and the **invoices** made from it, possibly several for one order (partial delivery, Q-02). An invoice fixes its prices, discounts and GST when it is made (a snapshot, BR-PRC-03), takes the stock out through the stock module and debits the school's ledger through the ledger; it is never edited, only cancelled, which reverses both (BR-LED-05).

Feature **School order and invoice** (R1-R18). Later features of this module: invoice from samples (§4.13).

Feature **Invoice PDF** (R28-R33, Draft): the printed A4 tax invoice of an issued invoice — the company's identity in the header, the lines, a rate-wise GST summary (the GST on the total, never a per-book column), the totals in words, the bank details, the terms and conditions and the signature, across as many pages as the lines need with the header repeated on each.

Feature **School return (credit note and refund)** (R53-R69, built 2026-11-16): a school gives books back, optionally against an invoice; the credit note takes them into Main stock (good) or Damaged stock, credits the school's ledger the whole amount at the invoice's rate and discount, can be cancelled by the Owner and is numbered per financial year. The **refund** (R64-R69) pays the school's credit back in Cash, UPI or Bank and gives the school an A5 refund slip; the credit note PDF (R71) follows.

Feature **School counter sale** (R38-R52): a **counter** is opened for a school and a session; at it a **student** buys books and other items straight away, paid at once in Cash or UPI. The **counter bill** takes the stock out, never touches the school ledger (BR-LED-03) and can be cancelled by the Owner; students are recorded at the counter itself; the Owner reads the day's sales as a summary and prints the receipt. The shop's own direct customers ("Dukaan (Direct)", FR-CNT-14 to FR-CNT-22) and counter returns are later features.

Out of scope for this feature: the invoice PDF and print layout of the **other** documents (counter receipt, credit note, ...: their own modules later); adding lines from the class book list (FR-ORD-02, the Planning module); payments and their allocation to invoices (the Payment module); attachments on documents (owner's note, backlog); the Super Admin's screens in the `admin` app.

## 2. Concepts and glossary

- **Order**: a school's list of items and quantities for one session, first a **draft**, then **confirmed**; it is **closed** when nothing more will be invoiced from it.
- **Line**: one item of an order or invoice: the chosen **price version** (its name, MRP and rate), the quantity and the **item discount**.
- **Item discount / total discount**: a percentage or a rupee amount, on a line or on the whole document (BR-DIS-01, BR-DIS-02).
- **Taxable value**: a line's amount after its item discount and its share of the total discount (BR-DIS-03, BR-DIS-04).
- **Invoice**: what is delivered and billed now, from a confirmed order; numbered per financial year; **cancelled** but never edited or deleted.
- **Counter**: a sale point of the company at one school for one session (for example "Sunrise School Counter 2027-28"), R38.
- **Student**: a pupil of a school who buys at the counter; recorded at the counter itself, never imported (R40).
- **Counter bill**: one immediate sale to a student, paid in full in Cash and/or UPI (R42-R45).
- **Remaining quantity**: a line's ordered quantity minus what its live (not cancelled) invoices hold.

## 3. Business rules

**School order and invoice**
- **R1** Orders and invoices belong to exactly one company, the company of the session (platform P18-P21). Schools, items, prices, stock and the ledger are reached only through their modules' capabilities (school R16, R18; item R16; price R18; stock R11, R20; finance R5; company R16), never by joining their tables.
- **R2** An **order** has: a generated **number** per company and per **session**: `ORD/2027-28/0001` (a Jan-Dec school's session gives `ORD/2027/0001`), starting again at 0001 for each session, from the company code series with the prefix `ORD/<session>/` (platform P23, Q1). Orders for the next session are made before its financial year starts (February-March, BR-SES-04), so the number follows the order's session, not its date. The **school** (an active school of the company), the **order date** (the business date by default, not in the future), the **session** (school R18: filled from the school and the order date; the user may choose the session before or after it, BR-SES-06, Q9), **GST applied** (yes or no, starting from the school's GST default, school R5, BR-GST-03), the lines, the **total discount** and a remark (at most 500 characters).
- **R3** An order **line** has: the **item** (active), the chosen **price version** of that item (price R18; the item's default version on the order date unless the user chooses another, BR-PRC-02), and from that version the **MRP** and the **rate** (the version's sale price, Q4), the **quantity** (a whole number from 1), and the **item discount**: none, a percentage (0-100, two decimals) or a rupee amount (at least zero, at most the line's rate times quantity). One line per item and price version; adding the same item again adds 1 to its quantity (FR-ORD-03). At most 500 lines (FR-ORD-02). An item with no price version cannot be added (Q13).
- **R4** Items are added by name or code search or by **barcode** (item R16); a barcode that matches no item answers "item not found" and adds nothing (FR-ORD-04).
- **R5** **Low stock never blocks** (FR-ORD-08, stock R9): a line of a stock-managed item whose Main quantity (stock R20) is below the line's quantity shows a yellow warning; the order and the invoice are never refused for it.
- **R6** **Order lifecycle** (FR-ORD-09, Q2, Q11): a `DRAFT` can be saved at any point, changed freely and deleted. **Confirm** needs at least one line and makes it `CONFIRMED`; a confirmed order's lines, prices and discounts no longer change. A confirmed order becomes `CLOSED` when every line's remaining quantity is zero, or when the Owner closes it with a reason (5-500 characters), after which nothing more is invoiced from it.
- **R7** **The amounts** of an order and of an invoice are computed by one rule (BR-DIS-03 to BR-DIS-05, BR-GST-04, BR-GST-05), every step rounded to 2 decimals **half away from zero** (Q5):
  1. line gross = rate × quantity; item discount = gross × percentage / 100, or the rupee amount; line amount = gross − item discount;
  2. subtotal = the sum of the line amounts; total discount = subtotal × percentage / 100, or the rupee amount (at most the subtotal);
  3. the total discount is shared over the lines in proportion to their line amounts (BR-DIS-04); the paise left over by the rounding go to the line with the largest amount (the first such line on a tie); taxable value = line amount − share;
  4. with GST applied, each line's GST is on its taxable value at the item's GST rate, **exclusive** (on top, Q-01): when the company's state equals the school's state, CGST = SGST = taxable × rate / 2 / 100 each rounded; otherwise IGST = taxable × rate / 100 rounded (BR-GST-05); a missing state counts as the same state (Q6); without GST every GST amount is zero and the invoice shows no GST columns (BR-GST-06);
  5. total before rounding = the sum of the taxable values + all GST; **grand total** = rounded to the nearest rupee, half away from zero; round-off = grand total − total before rounding (Q7).
- **R8** An **invoice** is made from a `CONFIRMED` order (FR-ORD-10, FR-ORD-11, Q2): the user enters, for each line, the quantity delivered now (by default its remaining quantity; never more than it; at least one line above zero). The invoice copies the order's school, session and GST choice and each line's item code, name, HSN, GST rate, price version name, MRP, rate and item discount; a percentage discount applies as it is, a rupee discount (item or total) is shared by the delivered quantity (item) or by the invoice's share of the order subtotal (total), rounded as R7, and the last invoice that empties the order takes what is left (Q10). An invoice whose grand total is not above zero is refused.
- **R9** The invoice **date** is the business date by default; it may be earlier, never in the future and never before the order date (Q8). Its **financial year** is the April-March year of that date (finance R4).
- **R10** The invoice **number** is per company and per financial year: `INV/2026-27/0001`, `INV/2026-27/0002`, ..., starting again at 0001 each financial year, from the company code series (platform P23) with the prefix `INV/<financial year>/` (Q3). Every company has its own counter. A number is never given twice, also not after a cancel; a cancelled invoice keeps its number. An invoice made in February-March for the next session's order still takes the financial year of its date (BR-SES-02).
- **R11** **Making an invoice** posts, in the same request transaction: the stock out of every stock-managed line (stock R11: Main, `SCHOOL_INVOICE`, the delivered quantity; non-stock items are skipped) and one ledger transaction (finance R5, BR-LED-01): debit `SCHOOL_RECEIVABLE` (the school) the grand total; credit `SALES` the sum of the taxable values; credit `GST_OUTPUT` the sum of the GST; and the round-off on `ROUND_OFF` (credit when it is positive, debit when negative); the document is the invoice, its date the invoice date, its session the order's. The answer lists the items whose Main stock went to zero or below (R5).
- **R12** An invoice is **never edited** (FR-ORD-14). The Owner **cancels** it with a reason (5-500 characters): the stock and ledger postings are reversed (stock R11, finance R5) dated the business date of the cancel, the invoice becomes `CANCELLED` and keeps its number, and its quantities count again as remaining on the order (a closed order that becomes open again goes back to `CONFIRMED`, unless the Owner had closed it with a reason).
- **R13** **Snapshot** (FR-ORD-12, BR-PRC-03, BRD §8 decision 3): an invoice keeps the school's name, code, state and GSTIN, the company's state and every line's values as they were when it was made; a later change of a price, an item or the school changes no invoice.
- **R14** **Who does what** (Q3 of company D5, Q11 here): the Owner, Staff and an entered Super Admin create, change, confirm and delete draft orders and make invoices; only the Owner (and an entered Super Admin) closes an order and cancels an invoice. Anonymous callers get `401`.
- **R15** **Reading**: the orders and the invoices of the company, paged (page size capped at 200), searchable by number and school name or code, filterable by school, status and session, newest first; each school's profile shows its orders and invoices (platform U14, Q11). An order shows its lines with the remaining quantities and its invoices; an invoice shows its lines, discounts, GST, round-off, grand total and its money story (made, cancelled).
- **R16** Every create and change of an order or invoice is recorded who and when (the standard audit columns and the audit trail of `core`); the postings are the stock entries and ledger transactions themselves.
- **R17** Money is `numeric(18,2)`, percentages `numeric(5,2)`, never float; the rounding is R7's and happens only in the domain of this module.
- **R18** Every input is checked at the API boundary (lengths, ranges, at most 500 lines, whole quantities, two decimals) and the rules again in the domain; an order or invoice of another company is `404`, identical to an unknown id.

**Wholesale orders** (owner, 2026-11-15: "single single entry bahut tough hoga")
- **R19** **Orders menu**: the company app has a BUSINESS parent **Orders** with the order list (all schools, filters school, status, session, search) and the invoice list; "New order" first asks for the school, then opens the order editor. A school's profile keeps its Orders and Invoices tabs (the same documents). This changes platform U14 for orders and invoices.
- **R20** **A confirmed order can be changed** until it is closed: lines can be added; a line's quantity can be raised or lowered but never below its invoiced quantity; a line with nothing invoiced can be deleted and its price version and item discount changed; a line with something invoiced keeps its price version and item discount; the total discount and the GST choice can be changed only while nothing is invoiced. Every change recomputes the order (R7) and is audited (R16). An order whose remaining quantity becomes zero after a change closes (R6).
- **R21** Each order line may carry a **class** (one of the school's classes, school R6): the same item for two classes is two lines, so packing and later class-wise invoices follow the class; a line without a class is the school's general need. "One line per item and price version" of R3 becomes one line per item, price version and class.
- **R22** **Add class sets** (FR-PLN-01, owner's answers): on a draft or confirmed order the user chooses classes and the number of students of each (prefilled with the class's student estimate, planning R3); every book of each class's list of the order's session (planning R2) is added as a line of that class with quantity = students x quantity per student, at the item's default price version on the order date; an item with no price version or no longer active is left out and reported; a line of the same item, version and class already on the order is increased instead of doubled.
- **R23** **Bulk add**: many items chosen at once (filters: category, class, publisher/vendor, subject, search) are added with one quantity and an optional class, each at its default price version; the rules of R3 and R21 apply to each.
- **R24** Lines are edited in place on the order (quantity, class, version, discount, delete) with keyboard-friendly entry; the server recomputes after each save (R7). An order holds at most 500 lines (R3).
- **R25** Every refusal names the line it is about (item code and class) so a long order can be corrected quickly.
- **R26** **Invoice layout** (owner, 2026-11-15: "group kar ke dikha sakte hai ya kewal book ka list, both option"): an invoice line keeps the class of its order line. The same invoice is shown and printed in one of two layouts, without changing its data, numbers or totals: **by class** (lines grouped under each class with a class subtotal; lines without a class under "General") or **book list** (no classes; lines of the same item, price version, rate and item discount are shown as one line with the summed quantity and amounts; lines that differ in any of these stay separate). Each school has a default layout kept by this module (initially book list), set by the Owner from the school's profile; the invoice screen opens in the school's default and can be switched there and then.
- **R27** **Easy new order**: New order asks only for the school (search by name or code); the date is today, the session and GST come from the school (R2, R7) and can be changed; the first screen offers the three ways to add books side by side (class sets R22, bulk add R23, scan or search R24), and the order can be saved as a draft at any time.

**Invoice PDF** (Approved by abhishek, 2026-11-15)
- **R28** An issued invoice can be handed to the user as a **PDF** (FR-ORD-10, §6): the document is built as **HTML + CSS** from a template that lives in this module (Scriban) and rendered by the platform's shared renderer (platform P25, a headless Chromium). It is rendered **on demand** from the invoice and its snapshot (R13) and is **never stored**; a cancelled invoice prints too, marked `CANCELLED` (Q18). The answer is `application/pdf`, shown inline, named after the invoice (`INV-2026-27-0003.pdf`). When the renderer is missing or fails the answer is `500 document.render_failed` (platform P25) and nothing half-written is returned.
- **R29** The PDF follows the invoice's **layout** (R26): by class with class subtotals, or book list; the layout chosen on the invoice screen is the one printed, and the totals are identical in both.
- **R30** **Page**: A4 portrait, as many pages as the lines need, and **no wasted white space**: lines pack about 48 to a page (a 300-line school invoice must not run to hundreds of half-empty pages). The same compact **header is repeated on every page** (logo, company name, tagline, address, mobile, email, GSTIN when R20 allows, PAN, the title of R31, and the invoice number, date, order number and session); the **column header** of the lines repeats on every page; a slim **footer** on every page carries the invoice number, the school's name and `Page x of y` (FR-ORD-15). The party block is on the first page only. The totals, the bank details, the terms and the signature are at the bottom of the **last** page (R33), so the pages before it are full of lines (the owner: the account details are shown at the bottom). The **closing block** (rate-wise GST summary, totals, amount in words, bank details, terms and signature) is **never split**: when it does not fit under the last lines it moves whole to one more page that repeats the header, so the bank details and the terms are never lost, cut or separated from the totals (owner's question, 2026-11-15).
- **R31** **Title and header**: the title is **TAX INVOICE** when the invoice has GST applied; when it does not it is **CASH MEMO** (payment type `CASH`) or **CREDIT MEMO** (`CREDIT`, R34), each with a `GST Exempted` note (BR-GST-06) (Q16). The header (R30) carries the company's GSTIN (when R20 allows) and PAN. The party block (first page) carries the school's name, code, address, state and GSTIN (from the snapshot, R13), the **place of supply** (the school's state), the tax type (CGST + SGST for the same state, IGST for another state, none when GST is off) and the payment type.
- **R32** **Lines and GST**: a line shows its number, the item's name and code, HSN, MRP, rate, quantity, item discount and amount (rate x quantity - item discount; the total discount and the GST are in the totals, not on the lines). With the *by class* layout (R26) a grey band names each class with its subtotal after it and there is no class column; with the *book list* layout there are no bands. There is **no per-line GST column** (the owner: "GST total ka hota hai, per book nahi"). When GST is applied it is shown once as a **rate-wise summary** (one row per HSN and rate: taxable value, CGST + SGST or IGST, tax) and the totals are subtotal, total discount, taxable value, CGST + SGST (or IGST), round-off and **grand total**; when GST is off there is no summary and no tax row: subtotal, total discount, round-off, grand total. The **amount in words** (Indian numbering) follows the grand total. The computation is unchanged (R7 computes each line at its item's rate; the summary only adds those up) (Q15).
- **R33** **Bottom** (the last page, one block, R30): the company's **bank account** (R19: account holder, bank, branch, account number, IFSC and UPI id; only the fields that are filled), the **terms and conditions** (R20, one numbered item per line of the text), the **footer line** (R20) and the **signature image** (R18) above a signatory line `For <company>` (FR-CMP-02/03, §6). The invoice **screen** shows the same rate-wise GST summary, totals and amount in words as the PDF, so the detail on screen and the print never disagree, and offers **Print** and **Download PDF** (sales#08).
- **R34** **Payment type** (owner, 2026-11-15, Cash/Credit memo): an order has a payment type, `CASH` or `CREDIT`, that starts from the school's default (school R20) and can be changed on a draft and on a confirmed order until something is invoiced (the same lock as the total discount, R20); an invoice keeps its order's payment type (R13). It decides the title of a GST-off invoice (R31) and the "Payment" line of the party block.
- **R35** **Dynamic terms** (owner, 2026-11-15: "invoice terms fix rahega, dynamic value hoga, each invoice nahi"): the terms and conditions stay **one fixed text per company** (company R20), never a text per school or per invoice, but the text may hold **placeholders** written `{{name}}` (spaces inside the braces are allowed) that are filled from the invoice when the PDF is printed: `company_name`, `school_name`, `school_code`, `invoice_number`, `invoice_date`, `financial_year`, `session` and `order_number` (the date as `15 Nov 2026`). A placeholder that is not in this list is printed as typed, so a typing mistake is visible. The terms text itself is the one the invoice kept when it was made (R13, R33), so a reprint never changes with the company's later edits; the placeholders take the invoice's own values.

**Item discount is a company choice** (owner, 2026-11-15: "discount har company ko nahi chahiye hota column pe ... agar on hoga to per column dikhega, nahi to final amount ka discount dikhega")
- **R36** Whether a line can carry its own discount is the company's setting `usesItemDiscount` (company R23, read through the company capability, never by joining its tables). **When it is off** (the default): an order and an invoice have **no item discount** anywhere: the order editor has no discount field on a line, the order, the invoice screen, the print and the invoice PDF have no discount column, and the only discount is the **total discount** of the whole document (R3, R7), shown as one line of the totals. A new item discount (percentage or rupee) on a draft or on a line of a confirmed order is refused `400 sales.item_discount_disabled`; a value a line already carries is accepted unchanged (so an order can still be saved). **When it is on** everything is as R3, R7, R20 and R32 say: the line has its discount and the documents show the column. **Documents already made** keep what they have (Q21): an order whose lines carry an item discount, and every invoice with one, still show the discount column and the same amounts whatever the setting now is; the column of a document **on the screen** is shown when the setting is on **or** one of its lines has an item discount. **The invoice PDF (the download and the print) is driven by the setting alone** (owner, 2026-11-15: "invoice download me bhi discount column setting se hi drive hoga"): the discount column is in it exactly when the company's setting is on at the time it is made, whatever the lines hold; the amounts never change. The computation (R7) does not change; an item discount of none is zero.

**Invoice directory for payments** (payment R1, R3, R6)
- **R37** The sales module offers one **read capability** for the payment module (and any later reader), never a join: for the session's company, the invoices of a school that are `ISSUED` (not cancelled) with their number, date, session and grand total, oldest first (date, then number), and the same facts for a list of invoice ids (the status included, so a cancelled one is known). It changes nothing and answers only the session's company's invoices.

**School counter sale** (owner, 2026-11-16: "school ke counter sale me customer nhi, student purchase krte hai"; Q24-Q33 answered one by one)
- **R38** A **counter** belongs to the company of the session (platform P18-P21) and to one **school** of it that is active and whose sale type is `COUNTER_SALE` or `BOTH` (school R4); a school that sells only by order is `400`. It has a **name** (2-150 characters, Owner-typed, for example `Sunrise School Counter 2027-28`), a **session** (school R18: the school's session on the start date by default; the Owner may choose the one before or after it, as for an order, BR-SES-06), a **start date** and an optional **end date** (not before the start). A school may have several counters, also in the same session. The dates are **information only**: a bill may be made on any day, inside or outside them (Q24: the counter opens when many students are still on leave).
- **R39** A counter is `ACTIVE` or `CLOSED`. A new counter is active. A closed counter takes no new bill (`409 sales.counter.closed`); its bills stay readable and cancellable; the Owner can open it again. The Owner changes its name, session and dates; the school cannot change once it has a bill. A counter is never deleted.
- **R40** A **student** belongs to one school of the company. It has a **name** (2-150 characters), a **class** (one of the school's classes, school R6, R16, stored as the school writes it) and optionally a **section** (at most 20 characters), a **roll or admission number** (at most 30) and a **parent mobile** (the same mobile rule as the other masters, school R10). Two students of a school are the same student when their **name (ignoring case and extra spaces) and class** are the same; the **section does not decide** (Q26: many students do not know their next session's section yet), so a section given later is stored on that student (the latest value) and a bill keeps the section it was made with. A student is created at the counter (R41), never imported and never deleted by this module; a student who is in a new class next session is a new record (the old one is still suggested by name).
- **R41** **Students at the counter** (FR-CNT-03): typing at least 2 letters of a name suggests the school's students whose name contains them (at most 20: name, class, section, roll number), for one school and optionally one class. A bill names the student by id, or gives name, class and the optional values: the student of that name and class is reused (its section and the optional values updated when given), otherwise one is created, inside the bill's request transaction, so a bill that fails creates no student.
- **R42** A **counter bill** has: a generated **number** per company and per **financial year**, `CNT/2026-27/0001`, starting again at 0001 each financial year, from the company code series with the prefix `CNT/<financial year>/` (platform P23, Q27), never given twice, also not after a cancel; the **counter** (active); the **school** and **session** (the counter's); the bill **date** (the business date by default, never in the future; its financial year is the April-March year of the date, finance R4); the **student** and **class** (both required, FR-CNT-02); **GST applied** (starts from the school's default, school R5, BR-GST-03, and can be switched on the bill); the lines; the **total discount**; the payments; and a remark (at most 500 characters). Several bills of one student are all kept under that student (FR-CNT-11).
- **R43** A bill **line** has: the **item** (active), the **price version** (price R18: the item's default version on the bill date unless another is chosen, BR-PRC-02), the **price** = that version's **MRP** (Q25: the school counter sells at MRP; there is no rate to type; money off is given only by a discount), the **quantity** (a whole number from 1) and the **item discount** (percentage 0-100 or a rupee amount, only when the company allows item discounts, R36; the total discount is always available). One line per item and version; adding the same item again adds 1 to its quantity. At most 200 lines. Items are added by barcode, by name or code search, by a **category button** for items without a barcode (item R3: pens, bags, ties and the other categories), or by **choosing the whole class book list** of the student's class in one step (planning R2: one line per book, quantity = its per-student quantity; FR-CNT-04). An item with no price version cannot be added. **Low stock never blocks** (R5): the bill warns, never refuses.
- **R44** The **amounts** are the rule of **R7**, the same one code path as an order and an invoice (D2): line = price x quantity - item discount, the total discount shared over the lines, GST per line at the item's rate when GST is applied (the company's state against the school's, R7 step 4), the grand total rounded to the nearest rupee half away from zero and the round-off shown; money `numeric(18,2)`, percentages `numeric(5,2)` (R17). A bill whose grand total is not above zero is refused.
- **R45** **Payment** (FR-CNT-07, Q30): the bill is paid at once in **Cash and/or UPI**: one or two payment lines (at most one per mode), each an amount above zero with two decimals, UPI with an optional reference (at most 50 characters); their sum must **equal the grand total exactly** (`400 sales.counter_bill.payment_mismatch`). There is no credit and no part payment at the school counter.
- **R46** **Making a bill** posts, in the same request transaction, the stock out of every stock-managed line (stock R11: Main, `COUNTER_SALE`, document type `COUNTER_BILL`, the quantity; non-stock items are skipped) and nothing else: **no ledger transaction** (FR-CNT-10, BR-LED-03; the payment is already in hand). The answer lists the items whose Main stock went to zero or below (R5). A refused posting rolls the whole bill back.
- **R47** A bill is **never edited**. The Owner (and an entered Super Admin) **cancels** it with a reason (5-500 characters, Q28): the stock postings are reversed (stock R11) dated the business date of the cancel, the bill becomes `CANCELLED` and keeps its number, and nothing is posted to any ledger. A cancelled bill is left out of the day summary (R50) and of the student's totals. A sold item that comes back is a return (R52), not a cancel.
- **R48** **Who does what** (Q32): the Owner, Staff and an entered Super Admin make bills and create students through the bill; only the Owner (and an entered Super Admin) opens, edits and closes counters and cancels bills. Anonymous callers get `401`; a counter, student or bill of another company is `404`, identical to an unknown id.
- **R49** **Reading**: the counters of the company (paged, searchable by name and school, filterable by school, session and status, newest first); the bills (paged, capped at 200, newest first, searchable by number and student name, filterable by counter, school, class, student, status and date range); one bill with its lines, discounts, GST, round-off, payments and the student (the low-stock warning is given when the bill is made, R46); and all bills of one student. A school's profile shows its counters (platform U14).
- **R50** **Day summary** (FR-CNT-12, Q29): for one counter and one date, or for the whole company and one date, the number of live bills, the total of their grand totals, the **Cash** total, the **UPI** total, the **number of books** (the quantity on lines of items of category Book, item R3) and the total quantity of all items. Cancelled bills are left out. It is a **read-only summary**: closing a day locks nothing and a bill may still be made for that day afterwards.
- **R51** **Receipt** (FR-CNT-09, Q31): a bill is printed as an **A5 PDF**, made like the payment receipt (payment R17) and the invoice (sales R28-R33), rendered on demand and never stored: the company header (logo, name, address, contact, the GSTIN when the company prints it, company R20), the title **TAX INVOICE** when GST is applied or **CASH MEMO** with a `GST Exempted` note when it is not (as R31, the bill being paid at once), the bill number and date, the counter and school, the student's name, class and section, the lines (item, quantity, MRP, discount, amount), the total discount, the rate-wise GST summary when GST is applied (R32), the round-off, the grand total and its amount in words, the Cash and UPI amounts, the company's footer and signature; a cancelled bill prints marked `CANCELLED`. Thermal-roll printing is a later addition.
- **R52** A **return** at the counter (BRD Q-06: yes, a small return with the money given back in Cash or UPI) belongs to the **Returns** module (BRD §4.12) and is not part of this feature. R16 (audit trail), R17 (money types) and R18 (input checked at the boundary, `404` for another company's record) apply to every counter, student and bill.

**School return (credit note)** (BRD §4.12 A, FR-RET-01 to FR-RET-09, with the refund slip; Approved by abhishek 2026-11-16, proposed by the assistant from the BRD; the refund FR-RET-09 and the PDF FR-RET-07 are later plans, Q34, Q38)
- **R53** A **credit note** (a school return, FR-RET-01) is made for one school: its lines are items the school gives back, and it may name the **invoice** they come from (optional). Credit notes belong to the company of the session (R1). It has a generated **number** per company and financial year, `CN/2026-27/0001`, starting again at 0001 each financial year, from the company code series (platform P23, FR-CMP-04), never given twice, also not after a cancel (R10's rule). Its **date** is the business date by default, may be earlier, never in the future and, with an invoice, never before that invoice's date (like R9); its **financial year** is the April-March year of that date and its **session** is the invoice's, or without an invoice the school's session on that date (BR-SES-03 to BR-SES-06).
- **R54** **The lines** (FR-RET-01, FR-RET-02, FR-RET-04, FR-RET-06): with an invoice, a line is chosen from that invoice's lines (never from a `CANCELLED` invoice, `409`) and copies the invoice line's item, price version, MRP, rate and **item discount**, so the rate is the invoice's (BR-PRC-04); the quantity is a whole number from 1. Without an invoice, a line is any active item of the company at the **last rate this school was invoiced for that item** (its latest live invoice line), or the item's default price version when there is none (Q35), and the Owner or Staff may change the rate. Each line has a **condition**: `GOOD` (goes back to Main stock) or `DAMAGED` (goes to Damaged stock, FR-STK-07). A line returning more than the invoice line sold, counting what live credit notes of that invoice line already returned, shows a **warning** and is never refused (FR-RET-06, like R5).
- **R55** **The amounts** follow R7 step by step, on the returned quantity and rounded the same way (2 decimals half away from zero, grand total to the nearest rupee, Q36): the **invoice's total discount is shared over the return in proportion to the returned line amounts** (FR-RET-03, BR-DIS-04), that is, a returned line's taxable value is its line amount minus the same share of the invoice's total discount that the invoice line carried; the GST is the invoice's GST rate and split (CGST and SGST, or IGST, R7.4). A credit note **without an invoice** has no total discount; whether it applies GST follows the school's default GST choice and can be switched on the document, like an order (Q37).
- **R56** The credit note has a **reason** chosen from `SESSION_END`, `DAMAGED`, `WRONG_BOOK` and `OTHER` (FR-RET-05, Q40), and a free remark (at most 500 characters, required for `OTHER`).
- **R57** **Making a credit note** posts, in the same request transaction: the stock in of every stock-managed line (stock R11: `SCHOOL_RETURN_GOOD` on Main for `GOOD`, `SCHOOL_RETURN_DAMAGED` on Damaged for `DAMAGED`, the returned quantity; non-stock items are skipped) and one ledger transaction (finance R5, BR-LED-01): **credit** `SCHOOL_RECEIVABLE` (the school) the **whole grand total, good or damaged alike** (FR-RET-08); debit `SALES_RETURN` the sum of the taxable values; debit `GST_OUTPUT` the sum of the GST; the round-off on `ROUND_OFF` the opposite way of R11's. The document is the credit note, its date the credit note date, its session as in R53. No money is paid out here: giving the school its money back is the refund (FR-RET-09, Q34).
- **R58** A credit note is **never edited**. The Owner **cancels** it with a reason (5-500 characters): the stock and ledger postings are reversed dated the business date of the cancel (stock R11, finance R5), it becomes `CANCELLED` and keeps its number; the returned quantities no longer count against the invoice lines (R54).
- **R59** **Snapshot** (BR-PRC-03): a credit note keeps the school's name, code, state and GSTIN, the company's state and every line's values as they were when it was made, like R13. Changing a price, an item or the school later changes no credit note.
- **R60** **Who does what** (BRD §3 permissions): the Owner, Staff and an entered Super Admin make credit notes; only the Owner (and an entered Super Admin) cancels one (R14's rule). Anonymous `401`.
- **R61** **Reading**: the company's credit notes, paged (page size capped at 200), searchable by number and school name or code, filterable by school, status, reason and session, newest first; a credit note shows its lines, totals, reason, condition per line and its invoice; the invoice shows the credit notes made against it and the quantity returned per line; the school's profile shows its credit notes (R15's rule).
- **R62** A return **gives the quantity back to the order** (Q39, answered yes by abhishek 2026-11-16): for a credit note made against an invoice, each returned quantity is subtracted from what that invoice line counts as invoiced, so the order line's **remaining quantity** (R-glossary) rises by it and the order can be invoiced again; a `CLOSED` order whose remaining quantity becomes above zero goes back to `CONFIRMED`, unless the Owner had closed it with a reason (as in R12). The invoice itself is never edited: it keeps its lines, totals and number. Cancelling the credit note takes the quantity away again (R58). A credit note without an invoice touches no order.
- **R63** R16 (audit), R17 (money types) and R18 (input checked at the boundary, at most 500 lines, `404` for another company's record) apply to credit notes unchanged. A credit note posts once and a retry never counts twice (finance R5, R15).
- **R64** A **refund** (FR-RET-09, Q34, Q41) is money the company pays **back to a school**, recorded by the Owner. It has: the school, the **amount** (above zero, two decimals, at most 9,999,999,999.99), one **mode**: `CASH`, `UPI` (transaction id, 4-50 characters, optional) or `BANK_TRANSFER` (UTR number, 4-50 characters, optional) (the modes of payment R5 without the cheque), the refund **date** (business date by default, never in the future), an optional **credit note** it settles (a credit note of the same school, live; the link is for the reader and limits nothing), and a remark (at most 500 characters). It has a generated **number** per company and financial year, `RF/2026-27/0001`, starting again at 0001 each financial year, from the company code series (platform P23), never given twice, also not after a cancel (Q44).
- **R65** **The limit** (Q42): a refund is at most what the company **owes the school**, that is, the school's credit balance (the balance of finance R7 below zero, as an advance or after a credit note); more is `400` and records nothing. The balance is read through the finance capability, never by joining its tables (R1).
- **R66** **Recording a refund** posts, in the same request transaction, one ledger transaction (finance R5, FR-LED-01 "Refund (Dr)"): **debit** `SCHOOL_RECEIVABLE` (the school) the amount; **credit** `CASH` for a cash refund and `BANK` for a UPI or bank-transfer refund (payment R7's accounts, the other way round); document type `SCHOOL_REFUND`, its date the refund date. The school's balance therefore rises by the amount. No stock moves.
- **R67** Only the **Owner** (and an entered Super Admin) records or cancels a refund (Q43); Staff get `403`, anonymous `401`. The Owner **cancels** a refund with a reason (5-500 characters): the ledger posting is reversed dated the business date of the cancel, the refund becomes `CANCELLED` and keeps its number; it is never edited.
- **R68** The **refund slip** (Q44): an **A5** PDF made like the payment receipt (payment R17) and the counter receipt (R51): the company's logo, name and contact, `REFUND SLIP`, the number and date, the school's name and code, the amount in figures and in words, the mode with its reference, the credit note it settles (when named), the remark, and a place for the Owner's and the school's signature; a cancelled refund's slip is marked `CANCELLED`. `GET refunds/{id}/pdf` answers it `inline`.
- **R69** **Reading**: the company's refunds, paged (page size capped at 200), searchable by number and school name or code, filterable by school, status and date range, newest first; the school's profile shows its refunds and the credit note's detail the refunds that name it (R15's rule). R16 (audit), R17 (money types) and R18 (input checked at the boundary, `404` for another company's record) apply to refunds unchanged, and a refund posts once, a retry never counting twice (finance R5, R15).
- **R70** (proposed by the assistant 2026-11-16 while building sales#23, to confirm) An **invoice with a live credit note cannot be cancelled** (`409 sales.invoice.has_credit_notes`): the owner cancels the credit notes first. Reason: a cancelled invoice gives its whole quantity back to the order (R12) and a credit note gives its returned part back (R62); both on one invoice would count the same books twice.

**Credit note PDF** (BRD §4.12 A FR-RET-07, §6; Approved by abhishek 2026-11-16, proposed by the assistant from the BRD)
- **R71** A credit note is handed over as an **A4 PDF titled `CREDIT NOTE`** (BRD §6 "Credit Note, School return, amount, A4"), `GET credit-notes/{id}/pdf`, `inline`, named `<number with / as ->.pdf`, rendered on demand and never stored, like the invoice PDF (R28-R33) and the counter receipt (R51): the company's identity as the credit note kept it (R59) with the live logo and signature, the number, the date, the school (name, code, GSTIN as kept), the invoice it names (when any) and the reason, each line (item, condition, rate, quantity, item discount, amount), the totals of R55 with the rate-wise GST summary of R32 when GST was applied, the grand total in words, the remark and the footer line. A **cancelled** credit note prints marked `CANCELLED` with its reason. The page number and the header repeat on every page when the lines need more than one (Q41). Everyone who reads credit notes may open it; another company's credit note is `404` (R63).

- `student` (aggregate root): `school_id`, `name`, `name_normalized`, `class_name`, `section`, `roll_number`, `parent_mobile`. Unique `(company_id, school_id, name_normalized, class_name)` on live rows (R40).

- `counter_bill_line` (child): `item_id`, `price_version_id`, item code, name, HSN, GST rate, item category, price version name, `mrp_amount`, `quantity`, `item_discount_type`, `item_discount_value`, and the computed amounts (gross, item discount, line amount, total discount share, taxable, CGST, SGST, IGST).

- `credit_note` (aggregate root): `number`, `financial_year`, `note_date`, `school_id`, school snapshot (code, name, address, state, GSTIN), company state, `invoice_id` (optional) and `invoice_number`, `session`, `is_gst_applied`, `reason`, `remark`, `status` (`ACTIVE`, `CANCELLED`), `cancel_reason`, the computed totals (subtotal, total discount, taxable, CGST, SGST, IGST, round-off, grand total), audit and concurrency columns. Unique `(company_id, number)`; index `(company_id, school_id)` and `(company_id, invoice_id)`.
- `credit_note_line` (child): `invoice_line_id` (optional), `item_id`, `price_version_id`, item code, name, HSN and GST rate, price version name, `mrp_amount`, `rate_amount`, `quantity`, `condition` (`GOOD`, `DAMAGED`), `item_discount_type`, `item_discount_value` and the computed amounts (gross, item discount, line amount, total discount share, taxable, CGST, SGST, IGST).
- `school_refund` (aggregate root): `number`, `financial_year`, `refund_date`, `school_id`, school snapshot (code, name), `credit_note_id` (optional) and `credit_note_number`, `mode` (`CASH`, `UPI`, `BANK_TRANSFER`), `reference`, `amount`, `remark`, `status` (`ACTIVE`, `CANCELLED`), `cancel_reason`, audit and concurrency columns. Unique `(company_id, number)`; index `(company_id, school_id)`.
