## 1. Purpose and scope

`vendor` holds the vendors and publications a company buys books and stationery from: who they are, how to reach and pay them, and the starting balance the company owes them. Purchase orders, purchases, returns, vendor payments and the vendor ledger of later modules point at a vendor by id and never copy these values.

Feature **Vendor master** (R1-R14): an Owner creates, edits, activates and deactivates the vendors of their own company; a Super Admin works with a company's vendors only after entering that company ([specs/company.md](company.md) R13). A vendor belongs to exactly one company and no other company ever sees it ([specs/platform.md](platform.md) P18-P21).

Out of scope for this feature: Excel import of vendors (FR-VEN-03, NFR-12: its own later feature, it needs an Excel package approved, Q8); posting the opening balance into the vendor ledger and vendor payments (the Ledger and Payment modules); purchase orders and purchases; the screens of the Super Admin in the `admin` app (Q9). The item master, which points at a vendor/publication, is the next module and uses only the vendor's id.

## 2. Concepts and glossary

- **Vendor**: one supplier or publication house of one company (R1).
- **Code**: the vendor's generated number `VEN-0001`, ... (R2, R11).
- **Bank details**: the vendor's bank account for payments (R5).
- **Opening balance**: what the company owed the vendor when the system was started (R6).

## 3. Business rules

**Vendor master**
- **R1** A vendor belongs to exactly one company, the company of the session that creates it. Another company's vendors are never read or changed (platform P18-P21); asking for one answers `404`, identical to an unknown id.
- **R2** A vendor has: **code** (generated by the system, never typed or changed, R11), **name** (required, 2-150 characters), address, contact person, mobile, email, GSTIN (optional) and state (FR-VEN-01). The state is chosen from the list of Indian states and union territories, because publishers are often outside Bihar and the tax logic of BR-GST-05 will need it (Q1). The optional values are trimmed and empty becomes none.
- **R3** The mobile number is stored as digits only (10-15 digits), an Indian number with a leading `91` on 12 digits or `0` on 11 digits is stored without it, the same way `iam` and `school` store mobile numbers. The email is checked for the usual shape and length. The GSTIN, when given, is 15 letters and digits in the GST shape and is stored in upper case.
- **R4** Two vendors of a company may not have the same name ignoring case and extra spaces (`409`), so the same publisher is not entered twice (Q10).
- **R5** A vendor has optional **bank details** (FR-VEN-01): account holder name, bank name, branch, account number (9-18 digits) and IFSC (4 letters, `0`, 6 letters or digits). All of them are given together or none. They are sensitive (CLAUDE.project.md): the account number is shown masked (only the last four digits) in lists, is never written to the audit trail, and the Owner reads it in full only on the vendor's own record; no other role sees bank details (Q4).
- **R6** The **opening balance** is an amount of at least zero with two decimals: what the company owed the vendor when the system was started (FR-VEN-02). Positive means the company owes the vendor (payable, the opposite of a school's balance) (Q5). It is stored here; posting it into the vendor ledger belongs to the Ledger module.
- **R7** A vendor is `ACTIVE` or `INACTIVE`. A new vendor is active. An inactive vendor keeps all its data and history but cannot be chosen for a new document (later modules); it can be activated again. A vendor is never deleted (Q7).
- **R8** The Owner creates, edits, activates and deactivates vendors. Staff have no access to vendors: a Staff account may only create orders and sell ([specs/company.md](company.md) D5, iam R68), so every vendor route answers `403` to Staff (Q3). Anyone else without a role listed here is refused (`403`, anonymous `401`).
- **R9** A Super Admin reaches a company's vendors only while they have entered that company ([specs/company.md](company.md) R13); with no company entered there is nothing to read and a write is refused (platform P19, P20).
- **R10** The list is paged (page size capped at 200), searchable by name, code, contact person, mobile and GSTIN, filterable by status and state, and sortable by name, code and created date (BRD §7.1: every list has search and filter).
- **R11** The **code** is generated when the vendor is created: `VEN-` and a number of at least four digits from a counter that belongs to the company (`VEN-0001`, `VEN-0002`, ...), from the platform's company code series ([specs/platform.md](platform.md) P23). A number is never given twice, also not after the vendor is deactivated, and two vendors created at the same moment get different numbers. The code is unique inside the company and cannot be changed. A `code` sent in a request is ignored.
- **R12** Every create and change is recorded who and when through the standard audit columns and the audit trail of `core` ([specs/core.md](core.md) A1-A9); the bank account number is never written to the old and new values of the trail (R5, the platform's audit secret marker). This module adds no audit mechanism of its own.
- **R13** Changing a vendor's name, state or GSTIN does not touch documents already issued: later modules copy what they must keep (BRD §8 design decision 3).
- **R14** The vendor's money rules: amounts use `numeric(18,2)`, never float; no rounding happens in this module (it only stores the opening balance).

## 4. Data

Schema `vendor`:
- `vendor` (aggregate root, company-owned, platform P18): `company_id` (not null), `code` (generated, R11), `name`, `name_normalized`, `address`, `contact_person`, `mobile`, `email`, `gstin`, `state`, `bank_account_holder`, `bank_name`, `bank_branch`, `bank_account_number`, `bank_ifsc`, `opening_balance_amount` (`numeric(18,2)`, not negative), `status`, plus the standard audit and concurrency columns. Unique index on `(company_id, code)` and on `(company_id, name_normalized)` among live rows.

`company_id` is a plain id with no foreign key across schemas (the `company` module guarantees it exists). The code counter is the platform's (P23), not a table of this module.

## 5. API contract (`/api/v1`)

All routes are company-scoped by the session (platform P19, P20); the company is never a request field.

| Method + route | Who | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `POST vendors` | `OWNER`, `SUPER_ADMIN` (entered) | `{name, address?, contactPerson?, mobile?, email?, gstin?, state?, bank?, openingBalanceAmount?}` -> `201` with the generated `code`; `400` for invalid values (a `code` in the body is ignored); `409` same name; `403 core.company_required` with no company |
| `GET vendors` | `OWNER`, `SUPER_ADMIN` (entered) | paged list (R10), account number masked |
| `GET vendors/{id}` | `OWNER`, `SUPER_ADMIN` (entered) | the vendor with its bank details in full (R5); `404` otherwise |
| `PUT vendors/{id}` | `OWNER`, `SUPER_ADMIN` (entered) | edits R2-R6 (not the code, not the status) |
| `POST vendors/{id}/deactivate`, `/activate` | `OWNER`, `SUPER_ADMIN` (entered) | R7; `409` when already in that status |

Errors are `ProblemDetails` with a message code from the module's catalogue. Validation failures are `400`.

## 6. User interface

Written in the UI plan; summary: in the company workspace (`member` app) the Owner gets a "Vendors" menu under MANAGEMENT: a list with search and filters; a vendor opens beside the list with an X to close it, and adding and editing open in the same panel (platform U10); activate and deactivate. Each state shows loading, empty, error with retry and success. Staff do not see the menu.

## 7. Events

Raised by the `Vendor` aggregate (past tense, ids and values only): `VendorCreated`, `VendorDeactivated`, `VendorReactivated`. Reactions: none in this feature.

## 8. Dependencies

`vendor` depends on `iam` for the current company (`ICurrentCompany`) and on `core` for the audit trail; it needs the company-owned data mechanism ([specs/platform.md](platform.md) P18-P22) and the company code series (P23, plan platform#07). Nothing depends on a vendor's code, only on its id.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

- **AC1** The Owner of company A creates a vendor: `201`, code `VEN-0001`, `ACTIVE`, `opening_balance_amount` 0; the row's `company_id` is A (R1, R2, R6, R7, R11).
- **AC2** A second vendor in A gets `VEN-0002` and the first vendor in company B gets `VEN-0001`; a `code` sent in the body is ignored; deactivating `VEN-0002` and creating another gives `VEN-0003`; ten vendors created in parallel get ten different codes (R11).
- **AC3** The Owner of B listing or opening A's vendor gets nothing or `404`, identical to an unknown id; a `companyId` in the query or body changes nothing (R1).
- **AC4** A negative opening balance, a 1-character name, a GSTIN of the wrong shape, a state outside the list and an invalid email are `400`; a second vendor named `ABC  publications` after `abc Publications` is `409` (R2, R3, R4, R6).
- **AC5** Bank details with only some of the five values are `400`; an account number with letters or 8 digits is `400`; a wrong IFSC is `400` (R5).
- **AC6** The list shows the account number masked (`XXXXXX1234`) and the audit trail of the vendor holds no account number at all; `GET vendors/{id}` as Owner shows it in full; Staff and other roles cannot read either (R5, R8, R12).
- **AC7** Deactivating a vendor keeps it in the list under the inactive filter; activating restores it; deactivating twice is `409` (R7).
- **AC8** Staff calling any vendor route get `403`; an anonymous caller `401` (R8).
- **AC9** A Super Admin with no company entered lists no vendors and `POST vendors` answers `403 core.company_required`; after entering A they list A's vendors and can create one (R9).
- **AC10** The list is paged and capped at 200, and the search, status and state filters and the sorts work; an unknown sort column is ignored (R10).
- **AC11** Every create and change of a vendor appears in the audit trail with the actor and company A (R12).
- **AC12** Mobile `+91 98765 00001`, `0 98765 00001` and `9876500001` are stored as `9876500001` (R3).

## 10. Decisions

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| D1 | New module `vendor` with its own schema. A vendor is a company-owned aggregate root. (proposed by the assistant) |
| D2 | The company code counter is a platform mechanism (P23, plan platform#07) so that school, vendor and the later documents share one rule instead of each copying the counter; school is moved onto it without changing its codes. (proposed by the assistant, Q6) |
| D3 | Vendors are deactivated, never deleted (R7), like schools, companies and accounts. (proposed by the assistant) |
| D4 | The vendor's opening balance has the opposite sign to a school's: positive means the company owes the vendor. (proposed by the assistant, Q5) |

## 11. Open questions

None. Q1-Q10 were answered by the owner on 2026-11-14 with the recommended answers ("approved hai kro implement"); the rules above follow them. The list below keeps the questions with the answer taken.

- **Q1** The state of a vendor: the school list holds only Bihar, but publishers are mostly elsewhere. Recommended: the full list of Indian states and union territories for vendors (R2); the school list stays Bihar only.
- **Q2** Vendor code prefix and format. Recommended: `VEN-0001`, generated per company like `SCH-0001` (R11).
- **Q3** Staff and vendors. Recommended: no access at all (R8), because Staff only create orders and sell. Alternative: read-only active vendors.
- **Q4** Bank details are sensitive. Recommended: stored with the five fields of R5, the account number masked in lists and in the audit trail, shown in full only to the Owner on the vendor's record. Alternative: do not store the account number, only holder, bank and IFSC.
- **Q5** Opening balance sign. Recommended: at least zero, positive means the company owes the vendor (R6).
- **Q6** The code counter. Recommended: a platform mechanism (P23, platform#07) that school also uses; alternative: copy the school's counter table for the vendor (faster, but two copies of one rule).
- **Q7** Delete. Recommended: never, only deactivate (R7).
- **Q8** Excel import (FR-VEN-03). Recommended: a later feature, after an Excel library is approved, together with the school and item imports.
- **Q9** The Super Admin's vendor screens in the `admin` app. Recommended: later, like the school screens.
- **Q10** The same vendor entered twice. Recommended: refuse a second vendor with the same normalised name inside a company (R4).
