## 1. Purpose and scope

`item` holds the things a company buys and sells: books, copies (notebooks), pens, pencils, bags, ties and others. It says what each item is, which vendor or publication it comes from, how it is taxed, whether its stock is counted, and which barcodes identify it. Price versions, stock, class book lists, orders, invoices and counter bills of later modules point at an item by id and never copy these values.

Feature **Item master** (R1-R15): an Owner creates, edits, activates and deactivates the items of their own company and keeps each item's barcodes; Staff can look active items up (to find them when selling); a Super Admin works with a company's items only after entering that company ([specs/company.md](company.md) R13). An item belongs to exactly one company and no other company ever sees it ([specs/platform.md](platform.md) P18-P21).

Out of scope for this feature: price versions, MRP, sale and purchase price (FR-PRC, the Price module); stock quantities and movements (FR-STK, the Stock module: this module only stores whether an item is stock managed); Excel bulk import and bulk update (FR-ITM-06, NFR-12: its own later feature, it needs an Excel package approved, Q9); class-wise book lists and planning (FR-PLN); the barcode scan flow of orders and counters (FR-SCN: it will look an item up by barcode through this module's read); the Super Admin's screens in the `admin` app (Q11).

## 2. Concepts and glossary

- **Item**: one thing the company buys and sells. A variant is a separate item ("Tie - Small", "Tie - Large", FR-ITM-05) (R1).
- **Category**: what kind of thing an item is: Book, Copy, Pen, Pencil, Bag, Tie or Other (R3).
- **Stock managed**: whether the item's quantity is counted. Book and Copy are counted by default, the others are not (FR-ITM-03, R4).
- **Barcode**: a scannable code or ISBN that identifies the item; an item may have several, or none (R6).
- **Vendor link**: the vendor or publication the item comes from, by id (R5).
- **GST rate**: the tax percentage that applies when GST is on for a document (R7).

## 3. Business rules

**Item master**
- **R1** An item belongs to exactly one company, the company of the session that creates it. Another company's items are never read or changed (platform P18-P21); asking for one answers `404`, identical to an unknown id. Two variants of one product are two items (FR-ITM-05); the name is not unique (many books share a name), the code is (R10).
- **R2** An item has: **code** (generated by the system, never typed or changed, R10), **name** (required, 2-200 characters), **category** (required, R3), vendor or publication (optional, R5), class (optional text, up to 50 characters, for example `5th`), subject (optional text, up to 100 characters), HSN code (optional, 4 to 8 digits) and GST rate (R7) (FR-ITM-01, FR-ITM-04). The optional values are trimmed and empty becomes none.
- **R3** The **category** is one of Book, Copy, Pen, Pencil, Bag, Tie, Other (FR-ITM-01) (Q1). It cannot be left out.
- **R4** **Stock managed** is a yes or no. A new item gets the default of its category: yes for Book and Copy, no for the others (FR-ITM-03, BR-STK); the Owner may set it either way. This module only stores it; counting stock belongs to the Stock module.
- **R5** The **vendor link** is the id of a vendor of the same company, or none (an own-brand item such as a bag has none, Q7). A vendor that does not exist in the company is refused (`400`). An inactive vendor may stay on an existing item but cannot be newly chosen. The vendor is read through the vendor module's capability ([specs/vendor.md](vendor.md) R15), never by joining its tables.
- **R6** An item has **zero or more barcodes** (FR-ITM-02). A barcode is 4-30 characters, letters, digits and hyphens, stored in upper case; there is no check-digit rule (Q5). A barcode is unique inside the company among live items: the same barcode on two items is refused (`409`), also when the other item is inactive; a barcode is unique per company, not across companies (platform P22). The Owner replaces an item's whole barcode list in one call; a barcode that is removed can be given to another item afterwards.
- **R7** The **GST rate** is a percentage chosen from a fixed list: 0, 5, 12, 18, 28 (Q3). A new item starts at 0 (books carry no GST, BR-GST-01). The rate is only the item's default: whether GST applies is decided per school and per document (BR-GST-02, BR-GST-03) by later modules, which use this rate when it is on.
- **R8** An item is `ACTIVE` or `INACTIVE` (FR-ITM-08). A new item is active. An inactive item keeps all its data and history but cannot be chosen for a new document (later modules) and does not turn up in Staff's list; it can be activated again. An item is never deleted (Q10).
- **R9** The Owner creates, edits, activates and deactivates an item and edits its barcodes. Staff may only read the list and an item and see active items only (so they can find what to sell, [specs/company.md](company.md) D5) (Q4). Anyone else is refused (`403`, anonymous `401`).
- **R10** The **code** is generated when the item is created: `ITM-` and a number of at least four digits from the platform's company code series ([specs/platform.md](platform.md) P23) (`ITM-0001`, `ITM-0002`, ...). A number is never given twice, also not after the item is deactivated, and two items created at the same moment get different numbers. The code is unique inside the company and cannot be changed. A `code` sent in a request is ignored (Q2).
- **R11** A Super Admin reaches a company's items only while they have entered that company ([specs/company.md](company.md) R13); with no company entered there is nothing to read and a write is refused (platform P19, P20).
- **R12** The list is paged (page size capped at 200), searchable by name, code, barcode, class, subject and the name of the vendor or publication (FR-ITM-07), filterable by category, vendor, class, stock managed and status, and sortable by name, code, category and created date (BRD §7.1: every list has search and filter).
- **R13** Every create and change is recorded who and when through the standard audit columns and the audit trail of `core` ([specs/core.md](core.md) A1-A9); this module adds no audit mechanism of its own.
- **R14** Changing an item's name, class, HSN or GST rate does not touch documents already issued: later modules copy what they must keep (BRD §8 design decision 3, price and item snapshots).
- **R15** Changing the **category** or **stock managed** of an item is allowed here; once stock or documents exist, the later modules refuse a change that would break their history (the same way the school's session type works, [specs/school.md](school.md) R3).

## 4. Data

Schema `item`:
- `item` (aggregate root, company-owned, platform P18): `company_id` (not null), `code` (generated, R10), `name`, `category` (text with a check constraint of the seven values), `vendor_id` (plain id, no foreign key across schemas, nullable), `class_name`, `subject`, `hsn_code`, `gst_rate_percent` (`numeric(5,2)`, one of the list), `is_stock_managed`, `status`, plus the standard audit and concurrency columns. Unique index on `(company_id, code)`; indexes on `(company_id, status)`, `(company_id, category)` and `(company_id, vendor_id)`.
- `item_barcode` (company-owned child of `item`, platform P18): `company_id`, `item_id`, `barcode` (upper case). Unique on `(company_id, barcode)` among live rows; a removed barcode is soft-deleted so its row leaves the list without a hard delete.

`company_id` is a plain id with no foreign key across schemas (the `company` module guarantees it exists). The code counter is the platform's (P23), not a table of this module.

## 5. API contract (`/api/v1`)

All routes are company-scoped by the session (platform P19, P20); the company is never a request field.

| Method + route | Who | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `POST items` | `OWNER`, `SUPER_ADMIN` (entered) | `{name, category, vendorId?, className?, subject?, hsnCode?, gstRatePercent?, isStockManaged?, barcodes?}` -> `201` with the generated `code`; `400` for invalid values (a `code` in the body is ignored); `409` a barcode already used; `403 core.company_required` with no company |
| `GET items` | `OWNER`, `STAFF`, `SUPER_ADMIN` (entered) | paged list (R12); Staff sees active items only |
| `GET items/{id}` | `OWNER`, `STAFF`, `SUPER_ADMIN` (entered) | the item with its barcodes and the vendor's name; Staff only an active item; `404` otherwise |
| `PUT items/{id}` | `OWNER`, `SUPER_ADMIN` (entered) | edits R2-R4, R5, R7 (not the code, not the status, not the barcodes) |
| `PUT items/{id}/barcodes` | `OWNER`, `SUPER_ADMIN` (entered) | `{barcodes: [..]}` replaces the barcode list (R6); `409` when a barcode belongs to another item |
| `POST items/{id}/deactivate`, `/activate` | `OWNER`, `SUPER_ADMIN` (entered) | R8; `409` when already in that status |

Errors are `ProblemDetails` with a message code from the module's catalogue. Validation failures are `400`.

## 6. User interface

Written in the UI plan; summary: in the company workspace (`member` app) the Owner and Staff get an "Items" menu under MANAGEMENT: a list with search and filters; an item opens beside the list with an X to close it, and adding and editing open in the same panel (platform U10); the Owner edits the barcode list there and activates or deactivates; Staff see the same list and detail read-only. Each state shows loading, empty, error with retry and success.

## 7. Events

Raised by the `Item` aggregate (past tense, ids and values only): `ItemCreated`, `ItemDeactivated`, `ItemReactivated`. Reactions: none in this feature.

## 8. Dependencies

`item` depends on `iam` for the current company (`ICurrentCompany`), on `core` for the audit trail and on the company code series (P23); it needs the company-owned data mechanism ([specs/platform.md](platform.md) P18-P22). It reads vendors only through the vendor module's directory capability ([specs/vendor.md](vendor.md) R15, plan vendor#03). Nothing depends on an item's code, only on its id.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

- **AC1** The Owner of company A creates an item with only a name and category Book: `201`, code `ITM-0001`, `ACTIVE`, stock managed yes, GST rate 0, no vendor, no barcodes; the row's `company_id` is A (R1, R2, R3, R4, R7, R8, R10).
- **AC2** A second item in A gets `ITM-0002` and the first item in company B gets `ITM-0001`; a `code` in the body is ignored; deactivating `ITM-0002` and creating another gives `ITM-0003`; ten items created in parallel get ten different codes (R10).
- **AC3** The Owner of B listing or opening A's item gets nothing or `404`, identical to an unknown id; a `companyId` in the query or body changes nothing (R1).
- **AC4** Categories Pen and Tie start with stock managed no and Copy with yes; an explicit `isStockManaged` overrides the default; a category outside the seven, a 1-character name, a GST rate of 7, an HSN of 3 digits and a class of 51 characters are `400` (R2, R3, R4, R7).
- **AC5** A vendor id of another company, an unknown vendor id and a vendor id of an inactive vendor on creation are `400`; a vendor of the company is stored and the item detail shows the vendor's name (R5).
- **AC6** An item with barcodes `8901234567890` and `abc-123` stores them in upper case; the same barcode on a second item is `409`, also when the first item is inactive; company B may use the same barcode; replacing the barcode list frees a removed barcode for another item; `["A1B2", "a1b2"]` in one list is `400` (R6).
- **AC7** Deactivating an item keeps it in the list under the inactive filter and hides it from Staff; activating restores it; deactivating twice is `409` (R8, R9).
- **AC8** Staff can list and open active items but `POST`, `PUT`, `PUT barcodes`, deactivate and activate answer `403`; an anonymous caller `401` (R9).
- **AC9** A Super Admin with no company entered lists no items and `POST items` answers `403 core.company_required`; after entering A they list A's items and can create one (R11).
- **AC10** The list is paged and capped at 200; search finds an item by name, code, barcode, class, subject and vendor name; the category, vendor, class, stock-managed and status filters and the sorts work; an unknown sort column is ignored (R12).
- **AC11** Every create and change of an item appears in the audit trail with the actor and company A (R13).

## 10. Decisions

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| D1 | New module `item` with its own schema. An item is a company-owned aggregate root; its barcodes are children of it. (proposed by the assistant) |
| D2 | The item code comes from the platform code series (P23), like school and vendor codes. (proposed by the assistant) |
| D3 | Items are deactivated, never deleted (R8), like the other masters. (proposed by the assistant) |
| D4 | The vendor of an item is read through a capability of the vendor module (vendor R15), not by joining across schemas, so each module keeps its own tables. (proposed by the assistant) |
| D5 | Prices are not on the item: price versions are the Price module's, so a price change never touches an item. (BRD FR-PRC, proposed by the assistant) |

## 11. Open questions

None. Q1-Q12 were answered by the owner on 2026-11-14 with the recommended answers ("all approved hai kro implement"); the rules above follow them. The list keeps the questions with the answer taken.

- **Q1** The categories. The BRD data model has an `ItemCategory` table (name, stock-managed default). Recommended: a fixed list of the seven categories of FR-ITM-01 for now (R3); a managed category list is a later feature if the owner wants to add categories.
- **Q2** Item code. Recommended: `ITM-0001`, generated per company like `SCH-0001` and `VEN-0001` (R10). Alternative: the owner types the code (BRD FR-ITM-01 lists "code" as a field without saying who makes it).
- **Q3** GST rates allowed. Recommended: 0, 5, 12, 18, 28 percent, default 0 (R7). Alternative: any percentage from 0 to 100.
- **Q4** Staff and items. Recommended: Staff read active items only (R9), because they must find what to sell. Alternative: no access.
- **Q5** Barcode format. Recommended: 4-30 letters, digits and hyphens, upper case, no check-digit test, unique per company among live items (R6). Alternative: only 8, 12 or 13 digit codes with the ISBN/EAN check digit verified.
- **Q6** Class and subject. Recommended: free text (class up to 50, subject up to 100 characters) for now (R2); the class-wise book lists of the Planning module will link items to a school's class names later. Alternative: choose class from a fixed list Nursery to 12th.
- **Q7** Vendor. Recommended: optional (own-brand items such as bags have no vendor); when given it must be an active vendor of the company (R5).
- **Q8** Stock managed. Recommended: default from the category (Book and Copy yes) and editable per item (R4, R15).
- **Q9** Excel import and bulk update (FR-ITM-06). Recommended: a later feature, after an Excel library is approved, together with the school and vendor imports.
- **Q10** Delete. Recommended: never, only deactivate (R8).
- **Q11** The Super Admin's item screens in the `admin` app. Recommended: later, like the school and vendor screens.
- **Q12** The same item entered twice. Recommended: no name rule (R1): books and variants share names; only the code and the barcodes are unique. Alternative: refuse the same name, class and vendor together.
